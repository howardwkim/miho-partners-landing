Write the body in plain markdown. Headings, lists, links, tables and emphasis all
work. `##` is the top level inside an article — the title is already the `<h1>`.

## What a section looks like

Body text is styled by `prose prose-miho`, which maps Tailwind's typography
plugin onto the site's own tokens. You do not need to add classes to anything.

Inline links work normally and can point at the [audit](/#offer).

Two characters need care, because the body is MDX rather than plain markdown: a
bare `<` and a bare `{`. Write `&lt;` for the first (`&lt;5 minutes`), and wrap
the second in backticks or escape it (`` `{placeholder}` ``). Anything already
inside backticks is fine as-is.

<Takeaway title="What to do about it">

Every article carries exactly one of these, near the end. It holds the concrete
two or three things a reader should actually do:

1. Something specific enough to start today.
2. Something with a clear yes/no outcome.
3. Something that compounds if they do it.

No import needed — `Takeaway` is available in any post. The `title` is optional
and defaults to "What to do about it".

</Takeaway>

Close with a short paragraph. Not a summary — a last thought that lands.
